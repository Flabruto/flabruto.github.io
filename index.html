Biography

Fausto Labruto is a radiologist, academic and author whose professional work combines clinical radiology, academic research, postgraduate education and international teleradiology.

His principal medical interests include emergency radiology, trauma imaging, acute abdominal imaging and the evaluation and implementation of artificial intelligence in clinical practice.

Alongside his medical career, he writes about ancient history and languages, with particular interests in the Hittites, the Etruscans and the civilizations of the ancient Mediterranean and Near East.
