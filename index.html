About

I am a radiologist, academic and author with a particular interest in emergency radiology, trauma imaging and the responsible application of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

Alongside my medical and academic career, I write about ancient history and languages, especially the Hittites, the Etruscans and the civilizations of the ancient Mediterranean and Near East.
